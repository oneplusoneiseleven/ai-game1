How the learning works

Each car casts rays and feeds the distances into a small neural network. The network outputs how hard to steer and how much to accelerate. A generation ends when every car has crashed or stalled. The furthest cars are copied as they are (the green ones), and the rest of the next generation is bred from the best by mixing their weights and adding small random changes.
